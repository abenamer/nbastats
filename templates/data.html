Update data

One click brings the current season up to date from the BALLDONTLIE API: the results and the schedule, with every Elo rating computed again; then the box scores of every played date not fetched yet and of the last days again; today's injury report; and the current rosters. Each step resumes where the last run stopped.

Seasons beyond the Elo dataset

The application computes every rating itself with FiveThirtyEight's formulas (25% reversion toward 1505 between seasons, K = 20 with the margin-of-victory multiplier, a new franchise at 1300), season after season from 1946-47. The archive supplies results and schedules through the 2024-25 regular season, and its own ratings stay stored as reference values; the application's ratings reproduce them to a mean difference below 0.01 points. Later results come from Update Data or a CSV upload.

Repeats the previous season's regular-season matchups one year later with no results, so the Season Forecast page can run a preseason forecast of a season the source has no schedule for yet.

CSV layout: date,home,away,home_score,away_score,neutral,playoff. Leave the scores empty for games not yet played. Team codes as in the Elo Explorer (OKC, GSW, …). Uploading again replaces the season's games that did not come from the archive; archive games always stand.
