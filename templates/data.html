Seasons beyond the Elo dataset

  
The Elo archive ends with the 2024-25 regular season. For later seasons the application computes ratings itself with FiveThirtyEight's formulas (25% reversion toward 1505 between seasons, K = 20 with the margin-of-victory multiplier), starting from the previous season's final ratings. These games are marked computed.
