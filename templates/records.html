The highest and lowest Elo ratings in the game log, 1946-47 to today. A team's rating after each game is the measure; "peak" and "low" are the best and worst single-game ratings of a season. Conference and division records use the mean pre-playoff rating of the teams. Before 1970-71 the league's two divisions (Eastern and Western) stand in for conferences. ABA teams are not aligned.

Roads to the title by era

A champion's road is the mean rating of its opponents before each game, over the regular season and the playoffs together (each phase weighs by its games). For each NBA/BAA era (the archive has no ABA champions), the champion with the hardest road and the one with the easiest; with one title in the era, both are the same.
