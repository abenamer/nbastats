Every team's chances of reaching the play-in, the playoffs, the second round, the conference finals, the NBA Finals and winning the title, simulated from the information available on a chosen date. Only games on or before that date are used; the rest of the regular season, the play-in and the playoffs are simulated. The night's games priced live are on the Tonight page, a team's report on the Injury card page, and the record against the sportsbook on Model vs Market.

Projected bracket

One bracket of very many. Each seed goes, in turn, to the team that held it most often in the simulated seasons among the teams not yet placed. The record is the projected final record, so a higher seed can show a worse record than a lower one. The chance beside each rating is the exact chance of winning that round, from the start of the playoffs, at the ratings of the cutoff date with fixed ratings. Each first-round series line is the exact series chance; a later-round slot shows the team more likely to have won the round before, marked projected. The simulation does not predict this bracket. The line under it says how rarely this exact seeding, or even this sixteen-team field, occurred.

NBA Cup

The in-season tournament, from 2023-24. The Cup games are told by the results feed, which labels each Cup game with its stage from 2025-26 on; the seasons before use dates recorded in the application, on whose group nights every game is a group game. The groups follow from the group games, which split the league into six groups of five. The group games are regular-season games, so the forecast already simulates them. For each simulated season the engine builds the group tables with the Cup's tiebreaks (wins, then the record among the tied teams, then point differential, with a simulated margin for games to come). It seeds the knockout with the three group winners and the best runner-up per conference. It plays the quarterfinals at the higher seed, and the semifinals and the final on a neutral floor. Played knockout games keep their result, and a knockout game already on the schedule keeps its simulated one. A knockout game not yet on the schedule feeds neither the standings nor the ratings.
