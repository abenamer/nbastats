What a player is worth to his team's rating, two ways. Worth reads the games he missed: each game's residual (the actual margin minus the margin the pre-game ratings expected) is regressed on the absences of both teams, with a prior that pulls every worth toward zero. Box reads his box score: Box Plus/Minus 2.0 and VORP from the season's lines, converted to the same Elo scale. The latest injury report priced with the first is what the Season Forecast takes off a team when you tick Use the injury report.

Return from absence

A report, not a forecast rule. A regular player who misses five or more of his team's games in a row and comes back is a return event. His box worth per game played, his minutes and his availability are read over the twenty team games before the run and the twenty after the return, in two halves of ten. The events are grouped by his BPM before the run and by the run's length. The control is every stretch of forty team games of a regular player with no such run, read the same way and grouped by the same BPM rule, so the drop two windows show without an injury can be taken off. Nothing here changes a rating or a penalty.
