The night's games and every team's rating

The full-strength rating replays the season's games with the Elo each side was missing in each game (players listed on that day's report who then did not play, at their worth) taken out of the expectation, so a loss played short does not lower it and tonight's absences come off the full team once; the stored Elo stands beside it in the team table. Each game takes two rows, as a sportsbook lists it: the visitor, then the home team after @. Each team has its chance to win, at full strength and at tonight's ratings, from the Elo curve with the regular-season home bonus: P(home) = 1 / (1 + 10^(−(home rating + bonus − away rating) / 400)), with no bonus on a neutral floor. The bonus is the Reg-season home adv. field when it is set, else the season's fitted value, and the note says which. Each team's spread is the same gap in points, 28 Elo to a point, written as a sportsbook writes it: − is the points the team gives, + the points it gets. The fair moneyline is tonight's chance as American odds with no bookmaker margin: −150 risks 150 to win 100, +150 wins 150 on 100; at a sportsbook the favourite must risk more to win 100 and 100 on the underdog wins less, so compare its spread directly and its moneylines after taking its margin out. Each column says whose number it holds: the model's (Model spread, Model moneyline, Model wins) or the sportsbook's, named for it and shaded. The sportsbook's lines come from The Odds API (the sportsbook of your choice, FanDuel by default) when a key is set, else from ESPN's scoreboard (DraftKings lines when ESPN carries them): its spread with the price of the bet, its moneyline, and its wins, the chance its two moneylines imply once its margin is taken out (each implied chance divided by their sum). Model − sportsbook is the model's chance on the night less the sportsbook's, in percentage points. A ★ marks a bet the model likes: a side's moneyline or spread whose expected return at the sportsbook's price (the model's chance of winning times the payout, less the chance of losing; the price carries the margin, so the bar is above it) reaches the bar in the field, five percent of the stake by default; the list under the table gives each bet's arithmetic. Spread chances take the model's margin against the line with the scatter of real margins measured from the played games. The edge is the model's disagreement with the sportsbook, unproven until the Model vs Market page's flagged record says otherwise. The team table lists every team by its rating tonight; a code opens the team's injury card. A what-if on a player's worth moves his team's rating by the difference from the model's estimate; the injury card takes its own. Nothing here averages over the season.
