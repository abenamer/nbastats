# Project terminology

These are project-specific technical terms. They are not a reproduction of the ASD-STE100 dictionary. The [REAS terminology](https://github.com/abenamer/reas/blob/main/standard/terminology.md) defines the general Rust and architecture terms (port, adapter, newtype, typestate, blanket implementation).

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Alpha | A franchise's ticket count in Engine A's title lottery. The title probability is alpha divided by the sum of all alphas; with every other franchise at 1 it is alpha / (alpha + N − 1). A count of chances, not a measure of the team. The Dynasty Lab labels it Title tickets. |
| Absence | A game of a player's team between his first and last game for it in the season that he did not play. The unit the worth is measured on; also, in a forecast, a rating penalty for a team over a span of dates, with an optional standard error the engine draws from. |
| Absence spell | A run of a team's games that a regular player did not play, read from the box scores. Its length, given how long it has lasted, is what the survival curve estimates. |
| Active player | A player the last roster refresh listed on a team. Stored as the `active` flag with the current team; every other player of the source is inactive. |
| Appearance | One player's box-score line in one stored game: team, seconds played, the counting statistics and plus/minus. Stored in `appearances`, keyed by game and player. |
| Bargaining period | A run of NBA/BAA seasons under one collective bargaining agreement, from the first season the agreement applied to. The table of periods and their changes is curated in `domain::cba`; see [Rule periods and bargaining periods](/concepts/periods.md). |
| Bookmaker margin | The amount by which a sportsbook's implied chances for the two sides of a game add up to more than 100%: about 4% on an NBA moneyline. Taking it out scales both chances to add up to 100%. |
| Box calibration | The share of a box worth that shows up as a team drop, fitted over every player with both a worth from the missed games and a box worth in the same season, with the error of the scaled box worth as a predictor of the drop. A line's box worth is scaled by it and combined with the player's own worth by precision. |
| Box Plus/Minus (BPM) | A player's value in points per 100 possessions above a league-average player, from his box score weighted by his estimated position and offensive role, with his team's players summing to the team's adjusted efficiency. Daniel Myers's published version 2.0, computed by the application. See [Box-score worth](/concepts/box-score-worth.md). |
| Box worth | BPM above replacement, times the player's share of the floor in the games he plays, times the team's possessions per game over 100, times 28 Elo per point. The box-score counterpart of the player worth, per game he plays. |
| Box-score fetch | The record that one date's box scores were fetched: the source, the time and the counts of lines received, matched and unmatched. A backfill skips the dates that have one. |
| Bracket | The 16 playoff slots and their pairings. Slots 0 to 7 are the East, 8 to 15 the West. |
| Champion z | The champion's pre-playoff rating above the league mean in standard deviations of that season's ratings; averaged over a group's seasons with a champion in the group records. Part of the parity profile. |
| Competition format | A period of the postseason and in-season structure: early playoffs, the 16-team field, best-of-seven throughout, the play-in era, the NBA Cup era. Curated in `domain::eras`. |
| Composition root | `src/main.rs`. The one place that creates the concrete adapters and wires them to the application. |
| Control sample | Forty team games of a regular player with no run of five, clear of the windows around any run of his, read in the same three windows as a return event and placed in a bracket by the same rule: what two windows show without an injury, for players like these. |
| Cup final | The NBA Cup (in-season tournament) final. An extra game that does not count in the standings. |
| Cup stage | The NBA Cup stage the results source labels a game with, stored with the game from the 2025-26 season on: one of six groups (`East Group A` to `West Group C`), a conference quarterfinal or semifinal, or `Championship`, the final. Each stage has a round, a conference and, for a group, the league's letter (`domain::models::CupStage`). The season engine tells the Cup games of a labelled season by them; see the [NBA Cup forecast](/concepts/nba-cup.md). |
| Current season | The season the daily sync targets: ends next year from September to December, ends this year from January to August. Also the playoff result of a team outside the play-in in a season whose playoffs have not started (`current` in the store, Current season on the pages): nothing is decided yet, so it is not a miss, and it ranks below one. |
| Decade | Ten seasons grouped by the start year of the season: 1959-60 belongs to the 1950s. |
| Dimension | A way to label seasons on the Records page: bargaining period, rule period, league size, league, decade, competition format, dynasty era, player era or themed era. A facet picks one label; a group query combines dimensions. |
| Draft prior | What a draft slot's rookies have been worth in their first season, pooled over the six seasons before: the mean box worth, the error of that mean and the spread per bracket (first pick, picks 2 to 5, 6 to 14, the rest of the first round, the second round, undrafted; a wider bracket stands in for a short one), which prices a rookie's report line until his minutes let his own box worth take over. |
| Dynasty era | A franchise's run of titles, at least a given number with no gap longer than a given number of seasons, from its first title to its last; computed from the champions. |
| Edge | A bet whose expected return at the sportsbook's posted price (the model's chance of winning times the payout, less the chance of losing) reaches the bar, five percent of the stake by default. The Tonight page marks it; the record scores the flagged bets apart. See [ADR 0010](decisions/0010-flag-a-bet-by-its-expected-return.md). |
| Elo | A rating that gives game probabilities from the rating difference. |
| Elo continuation | The computation of every rating by the application, season after season from the first season in the store. |
| Elo source | The origin of a game's ratings. Since compute first every game is `computed`; `imported` marks rows from an older version that the application recomputes at start. |
| Elo spread | The standard deviation of season-end ratings across a league in a season, averaged over a group's seasons. Wider means a more unequal league. |
| Engine A | The alpha lottery simulator (`domain::simulation::alpha`). |
| Engine B | The playoff bracket simulator (`domain::playoffs::bracket`). |
| Engine C | The full-season simulator with play-in (`domain::playoffs::season`). |
| Era | Consecutive seasons of one league with the same number of teams inside one rule period. |
| Era builder | The Records page's facets and group panel: the facets replay every table over the seasons of the picked eras (`GET /api/records?facet=`), the group panel computes one row of records per combination of dimensions (`GET /api/records/groups`). |
| Facet | One label along a dimension, written `dimension:label`, that keeps the seasons carrying it. Within one dimension facets add up, across dimensions they intersect. |
| Fair moneyline | A win chance written as American odds with no bookmaker margin. The favourite's line is the stake that wins 100 (−150). The underdog's line is what 100 wins (+150). At a sportsbook, the favourite must risk more to win 100, and 100 on the underdog wins less. Compare its lines after its margin is taken out. |
| Fetch window | The dates a results sync asks the source for: the whole season on the first sync, then the days from the last played game to today. |
| Franchise | The organisation, followed through relocations and renamings. Identified by `FranchiseId` and a key such as `GSW`. |
| Full-strength rating | A team's Elo with the absences of the games it played taken out: the season's games replayed with the Elo each side was missing in each game (listed on that day's report and absent from the box score) in the expectation. Tonight's absences come off it once. Computed in `domain::full_strength`. |
| Franchise of interest | On the Alpha Simulator, the franchise whose own probability of K or more titles is reported; `target_index` in the parameters. |
| Game log | The stored list of games with the application's Elo before and after each game, and the archive's reference ratings for archive games. |
| Game report | The wire-service recap of a played game (the Associated Press recap that ESPN hosts), linked from the game's score when the lookup finds one; stored by date and team codes. |
| Half-life | In the Alpha Simulator's season-dependent mode, the number of seasons for an alpha to move half of the way to 1. |
| Held forecast | The season run a second time with every absence that covers the cutoff date kept to the season's last regular-season game: the forecast if the roster of the cutoff date were the roster all season, the bound the dated forecast sits above. |
| Home-court bonus | Elo points added to the home team's rating in a game probability. The rating update and the playoff forecasts use 100, the archive's value; the regular-season forecasts of a season use the value fitted on the previous five seasons (`application::home_advantage`). |
| Implied alpha | The alpha a title record implies on the Alpha Simulator: the title chance per season that makes the titles the expected count (titles divided by seasons), as an alpha against every other franchise at 1. |
| Implied strength | The rating gap above the league mean at which a team of constant strength, against the actual playoff fields of a window of seasons, has the title chance a record implies; given in Elo points, points per game and wins per 82. Computed in `domain::strength`. |
| Information cutoff | The last date whose games a forecast may use. |
| Job | A stored simulation request with parameters, seed, versions, progress and result. |
| Injury card | One team's lines of the latest injury report priced with the arithmetic in the open: penalty per game, span, games covered, the part of the mean penalty and the expected wins lost to first order, totalled as listed and with today's absences held to the season's end. Its page is `/season/injuries`, with the season and the team in the address. |
| Injury report | The source's list of players with a status (out, day-to-day, questionable), the expected return date and a description, stored once per day under the day it was fetched. |
| Language model | A chat model with tool calling behind the `LanguageModel` port; the `OpenRouterModel` adapter calls it through OpenRouter's OpenAI-compatible API. |
| League size | The number of teams of one league (NBA/BAA or ABA) in one season. |
| Lean | In market tracking, the side whose win chance the model puts above the market's; scored as a one-unit bet at the sportsbook's moneyline. |
| Lookup | One tool call the language model makes to read the stored data (tool, arguments, rows). It goes through a store port or the application's caches. The question box lists the lookups under each answer. |
| Sportsbook wins | On the Tonight page, the column named for the sportsbook (FanDuel wins): the chance its two moneylines imply for a team once the bookmaker margin is taken out, its implied chance divided by the sum of both. Set beside the model's chance on the night. Named Market wins until 2026-11-02. |
| Margin scatter | The standard deviation, in points, of actual margins around the margins the pre-game ratings expected, measured over the played games of a season and its home-bonus window; the spread chances of an edge rest on it. |
| Market source | Where the night's sportsbook lines come from: The Odds API with the chosen sportsbook when its key is set, else ESPN's scoreboard, which is also the fallback. Each snapshot records its source. See [ADR 0009](decisions/0009-market-lines-from-the-odds-api.md). |
| Title futures | A sportsbook's outright prices on every team to win the title, read once a day through The Odds API and saved beside the model's title chance of the same day; scored against the champion once the season is decided. See [ADR 0016](/decisions/0016-track-the-title-chances-against-the-sportsbooks-futures.md). |
| Fair field | A field of outright prices read as chances without the bookmaker's margin: every implied chance scaled by the same factor so the field adds up to one, the margin reported beside them. |
| Market snapshot | One game's model numbers and sportsbook lines in the last capture before its start, with the model's version; scored after the result. See [Market tracking](concepts/market-tracking.md). |
| Backfilled snapshot | A market snapshot filled in from The Odds API's history for a night the live capture did not see: the sportsbook's lines as they stood before the tip-off, read a quarter of an hour before the night's first tip-off or before each, beside the stored pre-game Elo alone, under the model version `elo-stored-1.0`. The source is `the-odds-api-historical`. See [ADR 0018](decisions/0018-backfill-the-market-record-from-the-sportsbooks-history.md). |
| Paired comparison | In market tracking, the model against the sportsbook over the games both priced: per game the sportsbook's Brier score less the model's, its mean in standard errors, the games each was the closer on, the leader, whether the lead is decided at two standard errors from thirty games on, and the games it takes to get there. See [Market tracking](concepts/market-tracking.md). |
| Matchup (greatest, clanker) | A played game ranked by the combined Elo of the two teams before tip-off: the 20 highest are the greatest matchups; the lowest of each era, per league, is its clanker. |
| Mean Elo | The mean season-end rating across a league in a season, averaged over a group's seasons. A check that the ratings hold their level between periods. |
| NBA Cup | The in-season tournament, from 2023-24: six groups of five, a knockout of eight, a final on a neutral floor. The application tells the Cup games by the source's stage labels, or by a curated record of dates for the seasons before the labels, and derives the groups from the group games; see the [NBA Cup forecast](/concepts/nba-cup.md). |
| Parity profile | Season by season, the champion, the two finalists, the four conference finalists and the playoff field against their league: the gap to the league mean of the pre-playoff ratings in Elo points and in standard deviations (z), with the champion's rank; the mean z over a group of seasons is its parity figure. Computed in `domain::parity`. |
| PER | John Hollinger's Player Efficiency Rating: a per-minute sum of box-score events, pace-adjusted, with the league mean scaled to 15. Shown for recognition only. |
| Play-in | The games that decide seeds 7 and 8 (2020-21 and later), or the 8 seed (2019-20). |
| Player era | The seasons a listed player was active, from the curated table in `domain::players`; a season belongs to every listed player active in it. |
| Point spread | The expected margin of a game in points, written as a sportsbook writes it for each team: − is the points the favourite gives, + the points the underdog gets (NYK −9.9, BOS +9.9). On the Tonight page it is the rating gap with the home bonus at 28 Elo a point. Not the Elo spread, which is a standard deviation of ratings. |
| Possession | One team's turn with the ball. Estimated per game from both teams' shots, free throws, offensive rebounds, misses and turnovers; the unit the box-score rates are per 100 of. |
| Port | A trait in `application::ports` that the application requires from the outside world. |
| Projection | A placeholder season: the previous season's matchups one year later, no scores, reverted ratings. |
| Player source | The `PlayerSource` port: the box-score lines of one date, the current injury report, the whole player list and the current rosters from the BALLDONTLIE API. The adapter is `BalldontlieSource`, the same as the results source. |
| Player worth | What a team loses per game without a player, in points of margin and in Elo, from the ridge regression of the game residuals on the absences; with a standard error. See [Player worth](/concepts/player-worth.md). |
| Update | One run of **Update Data** on the current season: the results sync, then the daily player run (box scores, today's injury report, rosters), one update at a time, the player step queued behind any other player sync. |
| Player sync | One run of the player data fetch. It is a backfill of a season or of every season, the daily run, an injury report, a roster refresh or a load of the player list. The daily run covers the unfetched dates, the settling dates, the injury report and the rosters. One at a time. |
| Projected bracket | The most likely playoff bracket of a season forecast. Each seed goes in turn to the team that held it most often in the simulated seasons among the teams not yet placed. The bracket is priced exactly at the cutoff date's ratings and shown with the share of simulated seasons that had this exact seeding and this field. It is one bracket of very many, not the forecast. |
| Question box | The panel on the Dashboard that answers a plain-language question from the stored data through a language model with read-only tools, and declines every other question with a fixed sentence. |
| Reference rating | The archive's rating before or after an archive game, kept beside the application's rating for validation. |
| Result source | Where a game's result came from: `archive`, `results` (a results source or upload) or `projection`. Archive games always stand. |
| Results source | The `ResultsSource` port: game results and schedule of a season from the BALLDONTLIE API. The adapter is `BalldontlieSource`. |
| Road to the title | A champion's mean opponent rating before each game, shown for the regular season and for the playoffs (play-in included), and combined as the mean over every game, so each phase weighs by its games. Each era shows its champion with the highest road (hardest) and the one with the lowest (easiest). |
| Rule period | A group of eras that starts with a rule change: for the NBA the BAA's founding (pre shot clock), the 24-second shot clock (1954-55), the 3-point line (1979-80), zone defense (2001-02) and the hand-check ban (2004-05); the ABA is one period. |
| Scenario | A named, completed job that you can reopen and compare. |
| Season | An NBA season named by its end year. `SeasonYear(2004)` is 2003-04. |
| Season-dependent alphas | An alpha table with one row per season (`schedule` in the parameters), so a franchise's strength changes over the simulated seasons; the page builds it from a half-life. |
| Series score | The score of one played playoff series: the probability a model gave the actual winner against the actual loser with the actual home court, read as a two-sided Brier score, a log loss and whether the favourite won. Fifteen a season; defined in `domain::backtesting`. |
| Seed | Playoff position 1 to 8 inside a conference. |
| Series | A best-of-five or best-of-seven playoff matchup. A `SeriesState` is either in progress or decided. |
| Return event | A run of five or more of a team's games that a regular player did not play, with a played game before and after, with twenty team games before the run and ten after the return. The unit of the return-from-absence report. |
| Rating tonight | A team's rating as of the date less the penalties of the absences that cover the next night with a regular-season game: what the team plays at tonight, which the Tonight page shows beside the win probability at full strength and at those ratings. |
| Tonight | The page (`/season/tonight`) that prices the next night with regular-season games at the ratings the teams play at that night, beside the sportsbook's lines, with the bets the model likes. A panel of the Season Forecast page until 2026-11-05; the records before that date say Tonight panel. |
| Season menu | The entry of the top bar that opens the four season pages: Forecast (`/season`), Tonight (`/season/tonight`), Injury card (`/season/injuries`) and What if (`/season/whatif`). A tap or a click on its head opens and closes the list, so a touch screen can reach the entries; a mouse also opens it on hover. Model vs Market is its own entry. |
| Residual (game) | The actual margin minus the margin the pre-game ratings expected, at 28 Elo per point (FiveThirtyEight's spread rule for these ratings) with the ratings' home bonus. |
| Settling window | The last 2 days before today. The daily player sync fetches their box scores again, because a date fetched while a game was on holds partial lines. |
| Status curve | What a report status has meant on the stored reports: for each status and state (the player not yet out, or already in a run), the share of the lines whose player missed the team's next game and each of the next thirty. It gives the measured weight (the share missing tonight) and span (the games missed divided by the weight) that replace a status's defaults from 50 lines on. |
| Store | The `PostgresStore` adapter, which implements every store port. |
| Survival curve | The share of absence spells still going after each number of games, fitted on the runs of the five seasons before (Kaplan-Meier, with the runs cut off by a season's end counted for the games they lasted). It gives the expected remaining games of a run of a given length, which prices a report line whose return date is only the next game. |
| Threshold (K) | The title count that counts as a dynasty in the Alpha Simulator: the results report how often a franchise wins K or more titles. |
| Team | A team code and era, such as SEA or OKC. Identified by `TeamId`. |
| Themed era | A curated span of NBA/BAA seasons named for how the game was played or followed (the Reddit NBA era, the three-point dominance era); a season belongs to every theme active in it. |
| VORP | Value Over Replacement Player: BPM above the replacement level of -2.0 points per 100 possessions, times the player's share of the floor, times his team's games over 82. Times 2.7 it is wins over replacement. |
| Title HHI | The Herfindahl-Hirschman index of a group's titles: the sum of each champion's squared share. 1.0 when one franchise won every title; lower when the titles spread out. |
| New player | A player with no line in any stored season before the one played: a first-year player, one who lost his first season, or an arrival from abroad; a draft older than five years before the season bars the name, so a gap in the stored box scores does not make a veteran one. The rating has no record of him; the YAXEL backtest cuts the games by whether one played at least 24 minutes in his team's previous game. See [YAXEL](/concepts/yaxel.md), New players. |
| Absence-penalized rating | In the YAXEL backtest, the rating tonight read from the box scores in place of the injury report: the full-strength rating of the season's games replayed with the players who sat each game, less the Elo of the players missing tonight, read from the team's previous game (the roster's players who sat it) or from tonight's box score. A player is on a team's roster from his first game for it, or from the season's start when he ended the season before with the team, to the day before his first game for another team, or the season's end; each is priced at the worth he entered the season with. See [YAXEL](/concepts/yaxel.md), The absence-penalized rating. |
| YAXEL | The emergence term: a shift to a team's rating from the surprises of the players on its floor, each his recent box worth shrunk toward the worth he entered the season with by his minutes, less that worth, summed and damped. Named for the rookie whose first weeks the market priced and the rating did not. Backtested before use; see [YAXEL](/concepts/yaxel.md). |
| Management key | An OpenRouter key that can mint, list, update and delete the account's API keys and cannot ask a model; a provisioning key in the service's older words. `OPENROUTER_MANAGEMENT_KEY` turns the key rotation on. |
| Key rotation | The application minting its own OpenRouter completion key at start, replacing it on a schedule and when the service refuses it, and deleting the keys it minted before; see [ADR 0015](/decisions/0015-mint-and-rotate-the-question-box-key-from-a-management-key.md). |
| What if (page) | A real season run again with something changed, beside the plain run from the same date and seed: a rating set by hand, a team barred from the postseason, another playoff format, players out, a player's worth. See [ADR 0017](/decisions/0017-a-what-if-page-runs-counterfactuals-through-the-season-engine.md). |
| Barred team | A team the what-if keeps out of the play-in and the playoffs: it plays its regular-season games and keeps its record, and is ordered last in its conference, so the next team takes its seed. |
| Worth what-if | A reader's own worth for a player, typed from −400 to 400 Elo or read from his last 1 to 82 games through the box-score conversion: his team's rating moves by the difference from the model's estimate and his absences are priced at it, in the forecast, the Tonight page and the injury card, without changing the model. See [ADR 0011](decisions/0011-a-worth-what-if-moves-the-team-by-the-difference.md). |
| Tailnet | Your private Tailscale network: the devices logged in to your Tailscale account. Tailscale Serve opens the dashboard to the tailnet at `https://<machine>.<tailnet>.ts.net`, and to nothing else. See [Reach the dashboard over Tailscale](/runbooks/tailscale.md). |
| Opponent rating | The mean Elo of a team's opponents before each regular-season game it played, as the rating stood on the day: over every game (Opp. faced), over the wins (Opp. beaten) and over the losses (Opp. lost to), on the Elo Explorer's team table. The champion's version over the whole season, playoffs included, is the Road to the title. |
